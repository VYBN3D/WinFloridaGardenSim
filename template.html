HOW TO PLAY

1 · PLANT

Open MARKET, buy a seed (starting cash $20) and hit PLANT. Four pots per room — the board rotates you through one plant at a time, web edition included. New strains unlock after every harvest.

2 · WATER

Hydration drains every growth tick (3% — thirstier strains drain +2). Water packs add +32%. Below 20% the wilt timer starts; 20 dry ticks with no water and the plant dies. Bone-dry soil burns vigor fast.

$5 = 3 PACKS

3 · GROW

Plants grow only when hydration meets the strain's need and vigor is above 18. Normal speed lands a grow in about 25 real minutes; SUPPLY has FAST and WOW speeds. Stages: sprout → veg → pre-flower → flower → ripe.

NORMAL 30sFAST 10sWOW 4s

4 · PESTS

From 12% growth there's a small chance per tick of a pest swarm (gnats, mites, aphids). Severity climbs +7/tick, stressing vigor and thirst until you use a CARE KIT. Bad infestations warn you on any page.

$7 = KIT

5 · HARVEST

At 100% the HARVEST button lights up. A room yields 4 flower units graded by vigor into your stash, and unlocks the next strain in the catalog. Occasional mold events cost a unit — remediation kits buy them back.

6 · SELL & REPEAT

STASH shows untrimmed units and quality; selling pays units × (8 + quality/12). Roll credits into packs, kits and harder strains. 16 strains from Northern Lights to Foreign Kushmints.

7 · POWER

BOOT quick press opens POWER → quick press sleeps (screen off, grow continues), holding 1.5s deep-sleeps — a full reboot, exactly like the board. Tap the screen to cancel the popup.

8 · SAVES & UPDATES

Progress lives in this browser (localStorage) and the wall clock keeps growing while you're away. The web build tracks the firmware releases — OPTIONS → CHECK UPDATES compares your build tag against the latest GitHub release.

ABOUT THIS BUILD

WHAT IT IS

A browser port of the WinFlorida ESP32-S3 firmware (v42-S3): 480×320 panel, S3 wrapper geometry (×1.5 / ×1.33), the exact TFT_eSPI bitmap fonts (5×7 GLCD, 16px, 26px RLE), the leaf logo mask, the plant art pipeline and the IMA-ADPCM soundtrack decoded live to WebAudio.

HARDWARE ↔ WEB

ILI9488 panel → canvas · FT6206 touch → touch events · ES8311/FM8002E → WebAudio · BOOT → on-board button · light/deep sleep → suspend/reload · battery/charging → simulated · ESP-NOW Nearby → coming online. OTA → version.txt check.
